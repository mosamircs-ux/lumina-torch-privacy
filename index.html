Lumina Torch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Lumina Torch Android app (package com.luminatorch.flashlight) handles and why. Lumina Torch is a flashlight app. It does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server that receives data from the app.

1. Information the app uses on your device

  • Settings. Preferences such as brightness, strobe speed, Screen Light color, startup behavior, haptic feedback and the auto-off timer are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Camera flash. The app switches the device's camera flash hardware on and off to provide the torch. It does not capture photos or video and does not request the camera permission.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads, the SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (declared by the app with the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • an IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction and performance data.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). Ads are loaded over the internet, which is why the app requests the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. The app uses no other network features that we are aware of.

Lumina Torch is not directed at children. Ads may be shown to any user of the app. If you are under the age required by your local law to use this kind of service, please ask a parent or guardian before using the app.

3. Information we do not collect

Lumina Torch does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location for its own use, photos, microphone audio, browsing history or any other personal information for the developer. The app does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the code that was reviewed.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: load advertisements.
  • AD_ID: advertising identifier used by Google AdMob.
  • The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or account permissions.

5. Data retention and deletion

Settings stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes its stored settings. Data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies.

6. Security

Settings are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under normal Android protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
